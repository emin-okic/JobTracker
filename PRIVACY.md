# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

## Overview
Job Tracker (the "App") is a personal productivity tool that helps you organize and track your job applications. The App is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not require an account, we do not run our own servers for this App, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores on your device, how optional network features work, and the choices you have about your information.

## Information stored on your device
When you use the App, you can save information such as company names, roles or positions, application status (for example, Applied, Interview, Offer, or Rejected), dates, locations, links to company or job postings, and notes. This information is stored locally within the App’s storage on your device and remains under your control.

## Information we do not collect
We do not collect personal information. We do not collect analytics or usage statistics. We do not include third‑party advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission.

## Network features and third‑party services
Some optional features perform network requests directly from your device to provide results you request. For example, when you choose to look up a company address, the App sends your query to Apple’s map services through MapKit to retrieve place results. Apple may process your query and device information in accordance with Apple’s privacy policies. The App may also offer suggestion features for company names or job titles. Depending on how suggestions are implemented or configured on your device, your typed query may be sent from your device to a third‑party service in order to return results. We do not receive or store those queries on our own servers.

## Backups and sync
The App does not provide its own cloud sync. If you enable iCloud Backup or create encrypted device backups, your App data may be included in those backups and handled by Apple according to Apple’s policies. We do not have access to your backups.

## Permissions
The App does not request access to your precise location, Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone, Calendars, or Reminders. If a future version requires additional permissions to enable a specific feature, you will be asked to grant permission, and you can manage permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## Links you open
The App allows you to store and open links to job postings and company websites. When you open a link, you will visit that website in your browser or an in‑app browser, and that website’s privacy practices will apply.

## Data retention and deletion
Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual entries within the App, or you can delete the App to remove all data stored in the App’s container on your device. Deleting the App does not remove information that may already exist in your device or iCloud backups that you control.

## Security
Your data is stored within the iOS and iPadOS app sandbox and benefits from the security protections provided by the operating system, including hardware‑backed device encryption when your device is locked with a passcode. We do not implement additional encryption beyond what iOS provides. Keeping your device updated and protected with a strong passcode helps safeguard your information.

## Children’s privacy
The App is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in the App, please contact us so we can assist with deletion guidance.

## Your rights and choices
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Because your data is stored only on your device and not on our servers, you can exercise these rights directly by editing or deleting your data in the App or by deleting the App. If you contact us, we may receive your email address and the content of your message for the purpose of responding to your request.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, when possible, provide notice within the App or on our App Store listing. Your continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
